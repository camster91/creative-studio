These terms describe how Photogen plans, credits, and generated images work.

The service

Photogen is an AI image-generation tool operated by Ashbi Design. Images are produced by a third-party model provider (Google Gemini). Outputs can be inaccurate; review every image against your real product, approved claims, and destination requirements before publishing it.

Plans and credits

Paid plans are monthly subscriptions billed through Stripe:

  • Starter: $19/month for 100 credits
  • Pro: $49/month for 500 credits
  • Studio: $99/month for 1,500 credits

Each generated image uses credits based on its quality tier: Fast 1 credit, Balanced 1 credit, Quality 2 credits, Ultra 5 credits. A request for several variations uses the per-image amount for each variation. If your balance does not cover a whole request, it is refused and no credits are used.

Your credit balance is reset to your plan's monthly amount when each billing period renews. Unused credits do not roll over. Credits have no cash value.

Credits for images that fail to generate, or that are not produced because you cancel a batch, are returned to your balance automatically. Checking job status and viewing history does not use credits.

Free trial credits

New accounts receive 5 free trial credits, used at the same per-image rates.

Your own API key

If you add your own Gemini API key, generations made with it do not use Photogen credits. Google bills that usage to your Google account under Google's terms.

Cancelling

You can manage or cancel your subscription from Settings → Billing, which opens the Stripe customer portal.

Acceptable use

Do not use Photogen to create unlawful, infringing, deceptive, or harmful content, or content that violates the model provider's usage policies. We may suspend accounts that do.

Privacy

How we handle your data is described in the Privacy Policy.
