Photoroom vs PhotoGen

Both make product photos. They're built for different jobs. Photoroom is a fast editor for cut-outs and clean backgrounds. PhotoGen makes whole campaign sets from your brand rules and product facts. Here's how to pick.

Pick Photoroom if you mostly need

  • Background removal for product-page PNGs
  • Quick edits to a photo you already have, like swapping the background
  • A mature editor with lots of ready-made templates

Pick PhotoGen if you need

  • Lifestyle and ad scenes, not just a cut-out
  • One brief that returns every channel size you sell in
  • Brand rules and approved claims applied to every image
  • A review step before anything goes live

What neither tool does for you

  • Pack-level sign-off. AI can get small label details wrong. Check every image against the real pack before you publish, whichever tool made it.
  • Fine colour control. "This exact colour, slightly warmer" is still easier in a photo editor. Both tools trade some control for speed.
  • Tricky materials. Hair, fur and translucent packaging are hard edge cases for generative tools.

Many brands use both

Photoroom for the product page cut-outs, PhotoGen for the launch campaign. If your week is mostly ads, social and email, start with PhotoGen. If it's mostly product-page PNGs, start with Photoroom.
