Bring your own Gemini key

Add your own Google Gemini API key in the app and generation doesn't use credits. Google bills your key directly at its own rates. Your key is stored in your browser, not in PhotoGen's database.

Pricing questions

What is a credit?

Credits pay for generated images. Fast and Balanced images cost 1 credit each, Quality images 2 and Ultra images 5.

Do unused credits roll over?

No. Your balance resets to your plan's monthly credits at the start of each billing cycle.

Can I generate without credits?

Yes. Add your own Gemini API key in the app and generation doesn't use credits. Google bills your key directly.

How do I cancel?

Plans are monthly. Cancel anytime from the billing settings in the app, which open the Stripe customer portal.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. Sign up with an email link and you get 5 free credits.
